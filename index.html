How the benchmark is builtScenario → graph → references & instruction → checklist

Experts start from realistic scenarios and construct an entity–relation graph. The same graph aligns reference inventories, synthesis instructions, and atomic evaluation criteria. At least 30 cases include two or more hard-negative references; development and quality-check cases are excluded from the reported 90-case benchmark.

Inside LongBanana-EvalItem-specific evidence, tools, and auditable judgments

Each checklist item combines the candidate image, relevant references, allowed ordinal labels, and the graph unit being judged. Three critics assess content, relationships, and global constraints, with optional OCR, counting, layout, and identity tools. Every judgment records an evidence trace before aggregation.

THE CONTROLLER MATTERS

RPR improves checklist averages across the reported harnesses, but task pass rates decrease for OpenCode and Hermes-Agent. Better reasoning must preserve the correct reference bindings.

Evaluation protocol & human validation ＋

The human-audited pool covers 90 cases and 10 generation conditions: 900 outputs and 46,700 reviewed checklist labels. Subclass and overall scores use checklist averages; suite averages are the mean of their three subclass scores. The independently double-labeled subset has 89.4% exact agreement and 0.83 quadratic weighted kappa.
